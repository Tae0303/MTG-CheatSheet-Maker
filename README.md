# Deck Keyword Booklet

Paste a Magic: The Gathering decklist and get a printable cheat sheet of every keyword in the deck: keywords, ability words, tokens and counters, each with a short explanation. Every panel is exactly standard card size (63 × 88 mm) and styled like a Magic card, with your commander's art on the cover.

## Four ways to use it

- **Cards:** separate cards, 9 per page. Cut them out and sleeve them with your deck.
- **Accordion:** print one-sided, glue the strips together and fold them like a fan.
- **Mini booklet:** print double-sided (flip on long edge), fold the sheets and staple along the fold.
- **Image:** save every card as one sharp picture (PNG) to keep on your phone or tablet and zoom in.

For the first three, print at **100% / Actual size**, not "Fit to page", so the cards come out at the right size. Works on A4 and US Letter paper.

## Options

- Order keywords **by group** (make your own groups if you like) or **A–Z**.
- Untick keywords you don't need, or rewrite their text.
- Change the text size, or list the cards that use each keyword.
